## Rules in force since 1 October 2026
- Service messages are charged again (they were free since Nov 2024). Each business phone number gets
  1,000 free delivered service messages per month, reset monthly, no rollover.
- Utility templates sent inside an open 24h customer service window are charged as well (were free
  since July 2025).
- Free entry point (FEP) window: opened when a user messages from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad on mobile and
  the business replies within 24h; now up to **7 days** (was 72h) of free messages of every category
  except Meta Business Agent.
- Messages from users to the business stay free.

## Notes
- Meta's public marketing page still shows "service messages are free" and a "72 hours" FEP window —
  that copy is stale; the rate widget and the developer docs are the current source of truth.
- Template rules confirmed: utility must be non-promotional AND tied to the user's request, otherwise
  Meta auto-recategorizes it as MARKETING (status stays APPROVED). Edited approved templates are
  auto-approved unless review fails; max 10 edits / 30 days; category itself cannot be edited.
- Utility templates support up to 10 buttons (quick reply up to 10, URL 2, phone 1); >3 buttons shows
  the first two plus "See all options".
